Gym Rules & Etiquette

The Beast Code of Honor

Re-Rack Your Weights

Every dumbbell, barbell plate, and cable attachment is returned to its designated dock after your sets.

Wipe Down Equipment

Clean gym towels and sanitizer stations are provided throughout the floor. Respect the next athlete's hygiene.

Leave Ego At The Door

We support everyone from beginner to pro. Mutual encouragement and focused discipline define our culture.
